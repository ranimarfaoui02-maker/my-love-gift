A FEW THINGS I WANTED YOU TO KNOW

To Yassou ❤️

Maybe I don’t always know how to put everything I feel into words, but today I wanted to tell you something I probably don’t say enough.

Thank you for every beautiful moment we’ve shared, for every place we’ve been to together, and for every little memory that became special simply because you were there.

Thank you for every meal, every little surprise, every time you thought about me, and every single thing you did just to make me happy.

Maybe some of these things seemed small to you, but they were never small to me.

I noticed them.
I remembered them.
And I appreciated every little effort you made for me.

I love the little things about you, your presence, your smile, your way of making ordinary moments feel different.

And more than anything,
I love the memories we’re creating together.

I hope we never stop making them.
I hope there will always be another place to discover,
another meal to share,
another stupid laugh,
another beautiful day that becomes a memory.

Yassou, thank you for being part of my life, and thank you for every time you tried to make my days a little happier. ❤️
